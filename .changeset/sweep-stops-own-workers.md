In herdr `dw-sdlc sweep` stops and removes the terminals of the sweeping session's own Workers. It reads them as `worker ls` does, the terminals whose parent is its own, finished ones included, and every other live terminal with the folder it works in. A Worker whose status is none of working, blocked or unknown is stopped (`hc kill`) and then removed (`hc rm`) when its worktree goes in the same run, before the stale lock, the worktree and its branch; the worktree is judged whole first, so a Worker beside a dirty worktree is never stopped. A live Claude Code lock on that worktree that names the Worker's own program, the pid `hc get` reports, does not keep it: after the stop the lock is read again and lifted only when it is stale then, else the worktree stays and the line says so. Any other live lock keeps the worktree. A Worker whose folder is gone, with no worktree around it, goes under the same status rule, and a finished Worker is removed without a stop. Another session's terminal in a worktree keeps that worktree, named with the terminal's address and place and `hc kill` as the dev's command; the sweeping session's own terminal is never stopped. Terminals are items of kind `terminal`, `--json` gains `terminalsRead`, and outside herdr the output says no terminal was read. An `hc` that fails in herdr ends the Sweep with exit 2 before any removal; a stop that fails keeps the Worker's worktree with `hc`'s words and the Sweep goes on.
